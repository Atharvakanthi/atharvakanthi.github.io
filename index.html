Chennai hotel market, by zone

Eight micro-markets, five lenses. Pick a zone to zoom in and see how it prices, how guests rate it, and how hard hotels there have to discount. Values are indexed from zone medians.

Indexed from the Chennai hotel market report: price level = zone median rate relative to the highest zone; guest score = average review out of 10; pricing power = 100 minus average discount; leisure amenities = share of hotels with a pool relative to the highest zone; supply depth = share of hotels relative to the deepest zone.

Flagship and Multi-agent AI

Data Analyst Agent

A LangGraph framework that takes a business enquiry and returns a finished, reviewed market report. An intake agent splits the question into analysis buckets, a retrieval agent fills them from the internal database or requests missing data, analyst and business-analyst agents clean and shape the findings, a designer builds the report, and three full-read review rounds check every page and graph before delivery.

Result: report creation cut from one week to two days.

Data engineering and NLP

Location YouTube Intelligence System

An unattended eight-stage pipeline that tracks YouTube coverage and audience sentiment for real-estate micro-markets. It fetches incrementally, governs API quota, and runs a five-provider LLM cascade that flags low-confidence results so they never reach the aggregates.

Scale: 27,000+ videos, 1,700 channels and 200,000+ comments analysed across three markets.
