The flagship

An agent that writes the location report

I designed a multi-agent framework that takes a business question and returns a reviewed market report. These are real pages from its output, with names and sources removed.

  1. Intake and retrieval

    A business enquiry is split into a fixed set of analysis buckets. Retrieval fills each one from the internal database, or raises a data request and waits.

  2. Analysis

    An analyst agent cleans and shapes the findings. Every exhibit states how many records it rests on, and thin groups are marked rather than hidden.

  3. Business analysis

    A business-analyst agent turns the numbers into what they mean for each type of buyer, without recommending a purchase.

  4. Design and review

    A designer agent builds the page. A design reviewer checks rendering, then three full-read rounds check every page and graph before delivery.

  5. The finished report

    About fifty exhibits, a map, contents and a print mode. Report creation fell from one week to two days.

Comparable metrics

One metric set, three markets, a fair comparison

Defining the same five metrics for each market is what makes the comparison valid. Click a shape, or a name below the chart, to zoom into one market.

Content scale and audience reach are indexed to the largest market. Sentiment is the share of scored comments that are positive. Attention spread is 100 minus the share of views held by the top 1% of videos. Real-estate intent is the share of videos about land and property, indexed to the highest.
