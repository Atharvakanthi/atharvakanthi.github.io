ITC Fortune and Storii: Middle-Level Room Explorer

Monthly minimum, maximum, average and median rate for one room type at one hotel, with comparison charts. October 2026 to September 2027; rates captured 29 September to 2 October 2026.
How to read it. For each night we take the cheapest rate listed for the chosen room type (any plan). Min, max, average and median are then taken over the nights of each month. Middle level is ITC's "Premium" room tier (Premium Room, Executive Club), the step between Standard (basic) and Suite; use the room-type box to see Standard, Suite or Villa instead. A group such as "All Storii hotels" pools every hotel-night of its members. These are listed rates, not paid rates; no occupancy is in the data.
