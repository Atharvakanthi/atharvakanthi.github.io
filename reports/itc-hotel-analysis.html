What this data cannot separate

Which booking lead times exist in which stay months
15 of 72 cells contain any data
Filled cell = at least one rate listed for that combination.
One snapshot confounds season and booking lead time Rates were captured once (29 September to 2 October 2026), so booking lead time is simply the stay date minus the capture date. Nights 0 to 30 days ahead exist only in October 2026; 31 to 60 days only in November. The low October median (₹4,759 for Fortune) therefore reflects the month and the short lead time together. The portfolio-wide medians by lead band (₹6,239 for 0 to 30 days; ₹8,260 for 61 to 90 days) carry the same confound and are not evidence of last-minute discounting. A second capture a few months later would separate the two.
So whatFor 33 hotels the median list rate is flat from January to September 2027, but 15 of those 33 hotels re-price during the year, some by as much as 71%. No season-by-season conclusion is possible from one snapshot.

Sell-out signals

The availability file marks nights as sold out. Only 432 sold-out nights within each hotel's priced window are reliable signals; another 5,069 sold-out rows fall after a hotel's last priced date and are listing gaps, not demand, so they are excluded. A logistic model on October to December rows (5,904 observations, Monday as the reference day) gives:

Odds of a sold-out night versus Monday
Odds ratio; 1.0 means the same as Monday
Friday 1.60 (p=0.0016) and Saturday 1.87 (p<0.001) are clearly above Monday; Sunday 0.76 (p=0.054) is below. The model is weak (pseudo R-squared 0.011) and covers October to December only.

A relative revenue grid, not a forecast

If a hotel raised weekend rates and lost some weekend volume, the change in weekend room revenue is (1 + rate rise) x (1 - volume lost) - 1. The data does not say how much volume would be lost, so the grid shows the arithmetic for several cases. It applies to any hotel and uses no occupancy figure.

7. What this means

For hotel owners and sellers

A hotel's listed rate is best read against hotels of the same class and sub-brand: class medians run ₹6,090 to ₹10,400, and Fortune sub-brands run ₹6,650 to ₹9,005. Check which room tiers a comparison hotel offers, since Suites list 62% above Premium and Premium is missing at 7 hotels.

For investors and lenders

Rates for a full year are available for 33 of 66 hotels, so a 12-month rate view covers half the portfolio today. Listed rates are not revenue: occupancy and fee terms are not in the data. The operator's FY26 figures are in Chapter 6.

For revenue managers

55 of 66 hotels list no weekend premium, while the 11 that do range from 5% to 59%. Friday and Saturday sell-outs were more likely than Monday ones in October to December. Whether more hotels should price weekends depends on volume response, which this data cannot show (see the grid in Chapter 4).

For valuation and benchmarking work

Use class- and sub-brand-matched peers; use each hotel once; treat single-hotel classes (International, Wildlife/Desert) as unique; and treat Noida Sector 27 and Jawai as flagged levels. Repeat the capture before using the 2027 months for seasonality.
