Method. Videos and comments were collected through the public YouTube Data API for each market, classified into categories and scored for sentiment with a multi-provider language-model pipeline. Engagement data does not cover every video in every market, comments are the platform's top-ranked sample rather than a random one, and one channel dominates several categories.
