Second-home market

Sri Lanka Property Market

Three markets in one island: buying, renting long term, and renting short term to visitors. Everything here is a share of the market or an index against the national median (national median = 100), so districts compare cleanly.

Headline. Short-term rental is the largest channel in the sample, and it is concentrated on the southern coast. Land price varies more than tenfold between districts, and short-term rentals earn well in places with low occupancy, which is a reminder that price and demand are different things.

Method. Aggregates from a cross-section of online property listings in Sri Lanka, de-duplicated, with a minimum sample per district before a median is reported. Only shares and indices are shown. Listing prices are asking prices, not transaction prices. Short-stay occupancy is the platform's own estimate. Rent covers five districts with enough observations, so national comparisons for rent are indicative.
